# Introduction

---

### Train Your Focus with EaseStroop: A Cognitive Speed & Attention App

**EaseStroop** is a native Stroop Effect training app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, built on the classic cognitive psychology test used to measure attentional control, processing speed, and executive function.

The **Stroop Effect** is one of the most replicated findings in cognitive science: when the ink color of a color word mismatches the word itself (the word "RED" written in blue), your brain takes measurably longer to name the ink color — because reading is more automatic than color naming. EaseStroop turns this into a precise personal metric.

Run a session in under two minutes. Track your **Stroop Score** — a composite of reaction time and accuracy — across days and weeks. Stay motivated with **streaks**, **22 achievements**, and a **progress dashboard** that shows you improving. Choose from three difficulty tiers, four trial types, ten themes, and ten languages. Everything is stored locally on your device; no account required.
