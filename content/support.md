## Frequently Asked Questions

---

### What is the Stroop Effect?

The Stroop Effect is a well-established phenomenon in cognitive psychology: when the ink color
of a color word mismatches the word itself (e.g. the word "RED" printed in blue ink), it takes
measurably longer to name the ink color. Your brain's automatic reading response interferes
with the color-naming task. EaseStroop uses this to measure your attentional control and
processing speed.

### How do I start a session?

Tap **Start Session** on the Home tab. Choose your difficulty and trial type on the
pre-session screen, then tap Go. You can also enable a warm-up round of 5 unscored practice
trials in Settings → Skip warm-up.

### What is the Stroop Score?

The Stroop Score is a single number combining your median reaction time and accuracy:

`StroopScore = (1000 / medianRT_ms) × (correct / total)² × 100`

A higher score means faster and more accurate responses. A score above 60 on Interference at
Standard difficulty is a solid baseline.

### What are the difficulty levels?

- **Beginner** — 20 trials, 4 colors, no time limit. Interference trial type only.
- **Standard** — 40 trials, 6 colors, 3 seconds per trial. All three classic trial types.
- **Expert** — 60 trials, 8 colors, 1.5 seconds per trial. All four trial types including
  Reverse. Pro only.

### What are the trial types?

- **Word** — Read the color word (baseline reading speed).
- **Color** — Name the color of a colored rectangle (baseline color-naming speed).
- **Interference** — Name the ink color of a mismatched color word. This is the classic Stroop
  condition and the primary metric.
- **Reverse** — Say the word while ignoring the ink color. Expert / Pro only.

### How do I turn on Color-blind mode?

Go to **Settings → Accessibility → Color-blind mode**. The stimulus card and response buttons
will show a distinct geometric shape alongside each color, so the task doesn't rely solely on
color perception. Color-blind mode is always free.

### Will my progress sync across devices?
